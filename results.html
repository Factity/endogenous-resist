04

Looking at what actually lit up, token by token

feature_11_prompt_activations.json cooking_feature_28238_activations.json experiment_features_activations.json — 9 features × 3 prompts

Three ad-hoc inspection cells, run straight against the engine with no judge involved — just a forward pass through the model and the SAE. Two things stand out once you look at the raw per-position numbers instead of the summary max/mean.

Observation — feature 11's first 30 activation values are byte-identical across all three prompts (0.3086, 0.4912, 0.0, 0.3289 …), then diverge at position 30. That's the fixed Llama-3.1 chat-template preamble — date stamps and role headers inserted by apply_chat_template — not the prompts themselves. Whatever this latent is really tracking, its signal on prompt content only starts after the dashed line below.

Data-quality note — in both cooking_feature_28238_activations.json and experiment_features_activations.json, the tokens array is built by zipping a plain tokenizer(prompt) call (6–8 tokens, no chat template) against the trace from get_feature_activations() (36–38 positions, full chat template). zip() stops at the shorter list, so the token text shown below is paired with activations from the template preamble, not from the word it's labeled with.
